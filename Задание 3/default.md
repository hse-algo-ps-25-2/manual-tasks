*ABCD и E*

| Coin | ABCD | E   | ABCDE |

| 500  | 300  | 200 | 30    |

  ABCD = 300 (D = 200) -->
  ABC = 100 (C = 0) -->
  AB = 100 (B=100) -->
  A = 0**

| B      | 100   | 7       |
